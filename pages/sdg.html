The Sustainable Development Goals

The 17 SDGs provide a shared global framework for creating a better and more sustainable future.

SDG 1

No Poverty

End poverty in all its forms everywhere.

SDG 2

Zero Hunger

End hunger and promote sustainable agriculture.

SDG 3

Good Health & Well-being

Promote healthy lives and well-being for all.

SDG 4

Quality Education

Ensure inclusive and quality education.

SDG 5

Gender Equality

Achieve gender equality and empower everyone.

SDG 6

Clean Water & Sanitation

Ensure availability and sustainable management of water and sanitation.

SDG 7

Affordable & Clean Energy

Ensure access to affordable and sustainable energy.

SDG 8

Decent Work & Economic Growth

Promote inclusive economic growth and decent work.

SDG 9

Industry, Innovation & Infrastructure

Build resilient infrastructure and encourage innovation.

SDG 10

Reduced Inequalities

Reduce inequality within and between countries.

SDG 11

Sustainable Cities & Communities

Make cities inclusive, safe and sustainable.

SDG 12

Responsible Consumption

Promote sustainable consumption and production.

SDG 13

Climate Action

Take urgent action to combat climate change.

SDG 14

Life Below Water

Protect oceans, seas and marine ecosystems.

SDG 15

Life on Land

Protect forests, biodiversity and terrestrial ecosystems.

SDG 16

Peace, Justice & Strong Institutions

Promote peaceful societies and strong institutions.

SDG 17

Partnerships for the Goals

Strengthen global partnerships for sustainable development.
